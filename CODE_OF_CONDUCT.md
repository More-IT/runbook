# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Runbook welcoming, respectful and
safe for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or status.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes in good faith.
- Respect the project scope, privacy and security of Nextcloud users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualised conduct,
publishing private information without consent, and any other conduct that is
unwelcome or unsafe are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers at
`webmaster@more-it.co.mz`. Security vulnerabilities must follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This document is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
